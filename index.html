Situational intelligence blueprint

The planned full model evaluates efficiency, scoring margin, schedule strength, injuries, personnel, schemes, coach history, home/away, rest and bye weeks, weather, turnover luck, close-game volatility, over/underperformance, player workload, usage, shooting quality, and uncertainty.

Current engine: recent points scored/allowed, recency weighting, sample-size shrinkage, and home advantage. Player estimates blend available recent box scores. Other factors are not yet implemented in the calculations. Losing streaks, winless records, revenge, and being “due” never automatically boost a prediction.

Independent winner probabilities are not the same as betting value. Cover probabilities require a reference spread and are deliberately not generated by this engine.

Model validation

Before trusting probabilities, run historical out-of-sample backtests, check calibration and log loss, audit missing injury and player data, and compare against a simple baseline. Do not interpret the rankings as a proven edge.
